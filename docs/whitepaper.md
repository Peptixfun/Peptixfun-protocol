# Peptix Whitepaper

## Vision

Scientific curiosity should not need a gatekeeper. Peptix is a Solana token built around one idea: when questions, results and money move in public, more people can take part and fewer good ideas die in a drawer.

## Mission

- Keep the work visible. Every external fact the project relies on is recorded once, with its state, and is shown only when stated.
- Keep the market visible. The site carries a live board of the most active Solana pools, independent of any single token.
- Keep claims honest. Numbers on the site come from a live source or are left as pending.

## Roadmap

**Phase 1: Foundation.** Launch the site, publish the contract address once stated, open the community channels.

**Phase 2: Bench.** Open a shared space for questions, replications and peer notes.

**Phase 3: Desk.** Introduce software assistants that draft hypotheses and chase leads.

**Phase 4: Mesh.** Connect many small agents so results pass along a single pipeline.
